# S03: Player UI

**Goal:** Parents can control playback from their phone with a responsive, touch-friendly player view that shows what is playing in real time
**Demo:** Player view shows cover art, track title, controls work and reflect MPD state, volume slider adjusts smoothly, progress bar allows seeking, loads in under 3s on Pi

## Must-Haves

- Player view shows cover art, track title, artist/album, and "Track X of Y" on phone screen (375px width)
- Play/pause, previous/next, shuffle, and repeat controls work and reflect current MPD state
- Volume slider adjusts volume smoothly and shows current level
- Progress bar shows playback position and allows seeking by tap/drag
- UI in German with plain language labels, initial load under 3 seconds on Pi, JS bundle under 150KB gzipped
