# S06: Content Management

**Goal:** Parents can upload music, organize folders, and manage playlists through the browser without needing SMB or SSH
**Demo:** Drag-and-drop upload with progress works, folders can be created/renamed/deleted, playlists with drag-and-drop reordering

## Must-Haves

- Upload audio files via drag-and-drop with progress indicator, chunked upload within Pi memory limits
- Create, rename, and delete folders
- Create playlists and reorder tracks via drag-and-drop
- Assign cover art to a folder by uploading an image
